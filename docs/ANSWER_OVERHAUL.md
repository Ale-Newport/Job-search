# Application answering overhaul — 11 October 2026

This is the historical 11 October snapshot. See [Universal reliability verification](UNIVERSAL_RELIABILITY.md) for subsequent source repairs, installed acceptance checks, answer-history preservation and the authorized $20 budget.

This release separates verified factual reasoning from generative application writing. The overhaul is installed; the final live/native validation remains unfinished and is reported explicitly below. Failed drafts remain rejected, and coverage below distinguishes observed controls from attempts. No calibration application has been submitted.

## Demonstrated root causes

- The former extractive narrative composer and fallback paths reused project summaries instead of generating an answer for the exact question. Production form narratives now require `ApplicationAnswerLLM`; setup and provider failures cannot become generic text.
- Broad education matching confused secondary education with current university records. Explicit education levels and selectors now distinguish secondary, undergraduate, postgraduate, current and completed study.
- Generic date formatting and study-completion derivation manufactured a day from month-only evidence. The format resolver preserves precision, reads field instructions and rejects a day-level contract when the day is unknown.
- Checkbox choices were resolved individually, and native multiselect handling assumed a single option. The observer exposes groups; selection and final verification compare every selected and unselected member. A factual scalar such as one graduation year is represented as a one-item selection for a multi-choice control.
- Question descriptions, the exact approved CV and company text were absent or incomplete in writing context. Public pages rendered from hydration JSON could appear empty. Published JSON is now read as data, with source URLs, hashes and bounded caching.
- A landing-page search/chat heuristic accidentally excluded a narrative mentioning “job search.” It now matches only actual search-control labels. Applicant fields are separated from landing-page widgets before application entry navigation.
- Post-write acceptance did not always check the chosen radio label, explicit blank or native validity. Current diagnostics require observed values and revoke acceptance if a later site update changes a value or selection.
- A custom dropdown could contain typed search text without any selected option. Acceptance now requires an independently observed selection and a closed menu; search text is excluded from coverage.
- A Boolean enum code could select a decorated legal label that added unrestricted authorization or a specific visa route. Displayed labels now take precedence. Legal parsing preserves every requested authorization facet, jurisdiction and negative predicate; current permission cannot establish unrestricted, full-time, future, sponsorship-free or duration-specific permission.
- A privacy-notice reading acknowledgment was treated as processing consent. These are separate policies, including phrasing that asks the applicant to look at a notice and confirm that they have.
- Zero cloud token rates could bypass a zero spending cap, and cloned calibration databases maintained separate spending ledgers. Remote inference now requires an authorized positive cap and model-specific pricing; calibration shares the live ledger and current nonsecret configuration.
- A reference URL inside a role explanation was mistaken for a URL output contract. Source URLs are excluded from format instructions; a narrative textarea retains its prose contract.
- Saved Boolean consent did not map to exact “Yes, I consent” / “No, I do not consent” labels. These polarity equivalents are accepted only for the resolved consent question; extra purposes remain unsupported.
- Editable comboboxes were searched before their unfiltered menu was read, hiding equivalent displayed options. The observed combobox or its linked toggle now opens before search; the explicitly confirmed other-White category recognizes the displayed ethnic-background label.
- “If so” date choices failed to share their observed controlling radio, and sibling choices could be mistaken for the parent. The dependency now follows the parent and leaves all deadline dates blank after No.
- Real Monzo testing exposed additional semantic errors: course finish year, current study year and coding-experience explanations with optional portfolio links. These have dedicated general concepts and regressions.
- A dynamic work-status menu exposed the supported temporary-visa label only after opening. Once selected, its closed widget omitted the menu options and the resolver reused its earlier Boolean answer, repeatedly selecting the same choice. Closed custom selections now supply their independently observed label as an enum contract to verify against facts; search text cannot supply that contract. Geocoder reasoning retains the verified country even when options are present.

## Architecture and safeguards

`CandidateEngine` uses typed facts, versioned evidence and scoped policies for deterministic answers. New authoritative values supersede older imports without deleting history. UK current work authorization, permanent/full-time permission and sponsorship qualifiers remain distinct. A combined now-or-future sponsorship answer does not establish a future-only answer. The user's subsequent no-offer-deadline and AI interview note-taker Yes answers are scoped to the exact Palantir job. Name pronunciation and other distinct consents remain unknown.

`ApplicationAnswerLLM` supports OpenAI Responses, Anthropic, Gemini, native Ollama and compatible endpoints. Provider and model are configurable and discovered; keys use macOS Keychain. Primary and fallback models require their own current token rates. A zero cap blocks paid connection tests and generation before credential access or transport. QA clones reserve against the same live usage ledger, so repeated copies cannot reset spending. Requests carry immutable question IDs/context hashes, relevant professional evidence, exact field constraints, the actual job, and bounded company research. Not-on-resume requests additionally compare professional text from the exact linked approved CV version. The model cannot treat website text as instructions or use job requirements as candidate experience.

Structured outputs identify intent, supporting fact IDs, claims, exact selections and company sources. Deterministic evidence/format checks and a separate model review precede bounded regeneration. Omitted implementation citations may be restored only from verified evidence for the same supplied entity; answer prose is never changed by that reconciliation. The complete answer is checked independently of the model’s claim list. Undocumented incidents, diagnostic/repair actions, implementation guarantees, per-actor resource ownership, omitted historical implementation methods and causal methods are rejected against evidence for the same entity. Future hypotheticals remain distinct from completed history. A compact technical source index prioritizes documented tradeoffs, implementation and contribution facts without inventing a story or removing any authorized full-profile facts. Sourced employer dates and programme lengths are separated from candidate history; employer numbers cannot support personal claims. Repeated answers and structured role contradictions are reviewed across the application. Logging uses question/context/answer hashes instead of private answer bodies.

Native OpenAI budgeting prices ordinary input, cached input, cache writes and output separately for each exact model. Reservations use the highest applicable input rate; settlement treats cached and written tokens as subsets of total input. Missing or invalid usage cannot release a potentially incurred charge. Compatible endpoints retain their configured conservative input/output rates.

Only validated professional narrative rows can enter previous-answer context. Their projection excludes arbitrary metadata; contact, demographic, immigration and consent answers are excluded from generation, review and regeneration. The approved CV contributes its professional text after contact removal.

The frontend exposes secure provider onboarding, connection testing, usage limits, model settings and detailed answer inspection. At the user’s subsequent request, a saved complete-professional-profile mode now supplies all current verified professional facts and linked CV professional text on each narrative request. The observed current professional pool contains 179 facts across projects, experience, education, skills, languages and interests (60,706 serialized characters), with no private professional fields detected. It preserves English/Spanish and excludes the stale French import. This is request context, not model training or persistent provider memory. A 250,000-character profile overflow fails explicitly instead of omitting facts. Explicit full-profile requests have a separate bounded 1 MiB transport limit; ordinary requests retain 65 KB. Size rejection occurs before credential access or transport. The first full-profile replay exposed the older 65 KB boundary and made no candidate-context calls; its report is retained as a failed preflight, not answer-quality evidence. Opaque identifiers and metadata no longer distort content ranking; exact verified project-version names are treated as identity rather than outcome metrics. Generation runs asynchronously and reports per-question progress. A successful synthetic connection probe is explicitly separate from evidence of answer quality. Final form review independently re-evaluates observed education, dates, employment and legal facts against scoped knowledge, while narratives retain their own review. Failed searchable dropdowns stay unverified and require review; independent later fields can still be filled. A selected React Select chip is read independently of search text; a widget with several chips cannot establish one scalar answer.
